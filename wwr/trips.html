Our Rafting Adventures

Featured Experiences

Gentle Float - Family Paradise

Difficulty: Beginner

Perfect for families and first-time rafters! Our gentle float trips take you through scenic valleys with calm waters and stunning views. Learn the basics of rafting in a relaxed, safe environment while enjoying nature's beauty. Our certified guides ensure comfort and safety every paddle of the way.

  • 2-3 hour journey
  • Perfect for ages 5+
  • All equipment provided
  • Expert guide included

Moderate Rapids - Adventure Seekers

Difficulty: Intermediate

Ready for more excitement? Our moderate rapids course combines thrilling whitewater sections with beautiful scenery. Navigate Class III rapids and experience the adrenaline rush of real rafting while maintaining safety. Ideal for those with some experience or adventurous spirits looking to level up.

  • 4-5 hour journey
  • Ages 12+ recommended
  • Some prior experience helpful
  • Professional safety briefing

Extreme Challenge - Expert Rapids

Difficulty: Advanced

For the true adrenaline junkies! Our expert-level trips feature Class IV and V rapids that will test your skills and courage. Navigate some of the most challenging waters in California with our elite guide team. This is not for the faint of heart – this is the ultimate white water experience.

  • Full day adventure
  • Ages 16+ only
  • Advanced skills required
  • Elite guide team
